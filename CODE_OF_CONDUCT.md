# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is not reproduced here so that it cannot drift out of date. Read it at the link.

In short: participate with the assumption that other people are acting in good faith, critique the
work rather than the person, and accept that a maintainer's decision on scope is final even when
you disagree with it.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are
handled privately. The maintainer will respond within five business days.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: a private correction, a public correction, a
temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent ban. Reports about the maintainer's own conduct
should go to GitHub Support, since a single-maintainer project cannot investigate itself.

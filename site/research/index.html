Three claims. Different evidence.

Supported direction

Interface reach matters.

GUI access can make an otherwise inaccessible workflow actionable. Current product documentation and enterprise browser benchmarks support studying that gap.

Follow the evidence
Testable hypothesis

The best route is often hybrid.

Use structured tools where they fit, visual interaction where necessary, and one authority boundary across both. The economic advantage still needs a controlled study.

Inspect the study design
Not established

GUI use is required for AGI.

Acting in an environment and clicking a desktop interface are different claims. None of the cited results makes graphical interaction necessary or sufficient for general intelligence.

Examine the counterargument

Start by naming the model correctly.

The public OpenAI announcement names GPT-6 Astra, and compares it with GPT-5.6 Sol. It also describes a Codex harness update. That is evidence of a changing model-plus-system stack, not an ablation proving which part caused the gain. Read source S01.

Why long workflows deserve their own measurement

Illustration, not a benchmark35.8%

0.95 raised to 20, assuming identical independent steps and no recovery.

Real steps are not identically distributed or independent. Errors can be correlated; retries and verification change the process. This calculator explains why step accuracy is not workflow reliability. It does not estimate any product's performance.
